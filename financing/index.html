Commercial Truck & Trailer Financing — Built for the Trucking Industry

Securing the right commercial vehicle financing is one of the most critical decisions a trucking business makes. Whether you're an owner-operator buying your first semi-truck or a fleet manager adding units, Nationwide Haul works directly with NEF NOW — our sister company and a dedicated commercial vehicle lender — to give you fast, flexible financing options.

Credit requirements: While traditional lenders require a 680+ credit score, NEF NOW works with scores as low as 500. They evaluate business cash flow, time in business, and down payment as additional qualifying factors.

Terms range from 24 to 84 months. Pre-qualification is available with no hard credit pull — you get a real rate offer before committing.
