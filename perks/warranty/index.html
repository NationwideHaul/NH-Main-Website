Eligibility

In order for a truck to be eligible for a warranty it must meet the following requirements:

10 Years

Covered Component Options

Comprehensive protection across all major systems.

Engine

Internally lubricated hard parts, cam shaft, cam bearings, crankshaft, cylinder head and liners, intake and exhaust valves, main bearings, pistons and over 31+ additional components.

Critical Components

Complete injector, fuel pump, engine control module (ECM), water pump, turbocharger(s), and over 5+ additional components.

Aftertreatment System

Aftertreatment ECM (ACM), diesel oxidation catalyst (DOC), DPF dosing module, EGR valves, EGR cooler(s), SCR catalyst, wiring harness, and over 34+ additional components.

Transmission & Rear Axle

Internally lubricated hard parts of manual or automatic transmission, internally lubricated parts and power divider for differential.
