About Pitts Trailers

Since 1976, Pitts Trailers has been the world's largest and only complete-line forestry trailer manufacturer. From logging trailers to lowboy RGNs, tag-alongs, and marine chassis, Pitts builds every trailer to handle the most demanding jobs in forestry, construction, and heavy equipment transport.

Pitts is known as "the innovator, not the imitator" in the trailer industry, with patented technologies like the Loadpayin' Series frame, Lock N' Go landing gear, and military-derived rocking beam suspension — all engineered to haul more weight with less steel.

As an authorized Pitts Trailers dealer, Nationwide Haul offers the complete Pitts product line with competitive pricing, flexible financing, and comprehensive after-sale support for operations of every size.

Forestry — Log & Loader Trailers

Pitts pioneered the Loadpayin' Series in 1994, featuring a fully fabricated frame made of high-yield materials with continuous submerged arc welding. The result: trailers that haul more wood and less steel.

Log Trailer Models: LT40-8L, LT40, LT40-L, LP40-4L, PT22
Loader Trailers: KB48 with exclusive military-derived rocking beam suspension
Also available: Chip vans & moving floors with high-strength aluminum walls
