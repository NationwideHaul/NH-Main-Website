About Autocar Trucks

Autocar is America’s oldest motor vehicle manufacturer and the only American OEM dedicated exclusively to severe-duty vocational trucks. With over 125 years of innovation, Autocar designs and builds trucks from the ground up for the toughest jobs in refuse collection, concrete delivery, construction, and terminal operations — where uptime, durability, and driver comfort are non-negotiable.

The Autocar lineup is anchored by the DC-64 series — the first truck ever built with 160,000 PSI ultra-high-strength steel frame rails, 24% stronger and lighter than the industry standard, eliminating the need for frame liners in most applications. Alongside the DC-64 sits the legendary ACX® cabover for refuse, the ACMD® for medium-duty applications, and the ACTT® terminal tractor — including the all-electric E-ACTT with up to 22 hours of duty cycle per charge.

As an authorized Autocar dealer, Nationwide Haul offers the complete Autocar product line with competitive pricing, flexible financing options, and dedicated after-sale support for municipal fleets, private haulers, and construction operations nationwide.

Refuse & Recycling — ACX® & DC-64R

Autocar’s refuse lineup includes the ACX® cabover — the industry-leading low-entry cab for automated side-load and rear-load collection — and the all-new DC-64R conventional, the first new conventional refuse truck from Autocar in over 30 years. Both platforms feature severe-duty frames, ergonomic cab designs, and Autocar’s legendary reliability that keeps trucks on the route and out of the shop.

The DC-64R’s 160,000 PSI ultra-high-strength steel frame rails are 24% stronger and lighter than the industry standard, completely eliminating frame liners in nearly all refuse applications — reducing weight, cost, and maintenance.

  • ACX® low-entry cabover for refuse
  • DC-64R conventional with 160K PSI frame
  • Automated side-load, rear-load & roll-off
  • Maximum uptime & driver ergonomics

Concrete & Construction — DC-64 Series

The Autocar DC-64 series was purpose-built for the demands of concrete and construction. The DC-64M mixer, DC-64D dump truck, and DC-64P concrete pump are all built on the same ultra-high-strength platform with 160,000 PSI steel frame rails — delivering exceptional payload capacity, frame durability, and maneuverability on job sites where other trucks struggle.

Every DC-64 features a spacious cab with excellent visibility, tight turning radius, and a frame engineered to handle the extreme torsional loads of vocational work day after day without fatigue cracking.

  • DC-64M mixer, DC-64D dump, DC-64P pump
  • 160,000 PSI ultra-high-strength steel frame
  • 24% stronger & lighter than industry standard
  • No frame liners required

Terminal Tractors — ACTT® & E-ACTT

The Autocar ACTT® terminal tractor is engineered for the non-stop demands of distribution centers, ports, and intermodal yards. With a purpose-built cab for operator comfort during long shifts, heavy-duty drivetrain, and tight maneuverability, the ACTT is the go-to yard spotter for operations that need trailers moved fast and efficiently.

The all-electric E-ACTT takes terminal operations into the future with up to 22 hours of duty cycle per charge, significantly lower maintenance costs, zero emissions, and increased productivity — all without sacrificing the performance your yard demands.

  • ACTT® diesel terminal tractor
  • E-ACTT all-electric — 22 hours per charge
  • Zero emissions & lower maintenance
  • Purpose-built for port & DC operations
