MAC Trailers For Sale

Engineered for the Long Haul — Authorized Dealer

MAC Trailer is one of the leading manufacturers of trailers in North America. With over 30 years of experience, MAC has built a solid reputation for delivering high-quality, innovative trailers that meet the heavy-duty demands of the trucking industry.

As a primary partner, Nationwide Haul is proud to be your trusted multi-location dealer. Whether you're expanding your fleet or need flexible financing on a new unit, we have the inventory and expertise to get you on the road.

X-TENDER™ Technology

MAC's innovative aluminum extending frame system, allowing a single trailer to haul multiple load lengths. Built to increase versatility and reduce fleet complexity.

MVP MACLOCK™ System

A patented smooth-side panel locking mechanism on MVP dump trailers that eliminates traditional external posts, reducing weight and improving aerodynamics.

MAC Dump Trailers

Maximize your payload with our heavy-duty dump lineup, built for construction, aggregate, and scrap applications.

  • Standard Dump & Sheet/Post — Classic steel or aluminum end dumps for sand, gravel, and aggregate
  • MACsimizer™ Half-Round — Ultra-light aluminum at just 8,900 lbs for maximum legal payload
  • Steel Half-Round & Standard Steel — Heavy-duty steel construction for the toughest materials
  • Smooth Side "MVP" Dumps — Patented MACLOCK™ panel system, no external posts
  • Frameless & Frame-Type — Choose the right chassis config for your haul profile
  • Bullet™ Bottom Dumps — Precision material placement for road building & site work

Flatbed & Drop Deck Trailers

From the classic "Road Warrior" to advanced low-profile necks, MAC flatbeds deliver superior beam ratings, multiple axle configurations, and exceptional load stability.

  • "Road Warrior" Flatbed — Aluminum combo flatbed, 48–53 ft, the industry workhorse
  • Drop Deck & Step Deck — 40–53 ft lengths for oversized & over-height loads
  • MLP-6 & MLP-8 Low-Profile Necks — Reduced deck height for tall cargo with maximum stability
  • Conestoga & Side Kit Options — Retractable tarping systems for weather protection and fast load/unload
View MAC Flatbed Inventory See Flatbed Brochure

Pneumatic & Liquid Tanks

High-quality polished aluminum construction for both food-grade and industrial applications. Lightweight, high-cube frames ensure maximum payload with minimal towing effort.

  • High Cube Pneumatic (1650 / 1850 / 2000) — Maximum cubic capacity for dry bulk: cement, fly ash, flour, plastic pellets
  • 1000 Cube Pneumatic — Compact design for tight job sites and shorter routes
  • Vacuum Tanks — For liquid waste, oilfield services, and environmental cleanup
  • Liquid Tanks — DOT-certified aluminum tanks for fuel, chemicals, and food-grade liquids
View MAC Tank Inventory See Pneumatic Brochure See Liquid Tank Brochure

Transfer, Refuse & Tipper Trailers

Built for high-volume hauling of MSW, scrap, agricultural products, and recyclable materials. Aerodynamic designs reduce fuel costs and increase route efficiency.

  • Moving Floor & Live Floor — Automated unloading for refuse transfer stations and recycling facilities
  • Tippers (Sheet & Post) — Hydraulic tipping for fast dump cycles, ideal for scrap and demolition
  • Smooth Side "MVP" Transfer — Aerodynamic MACLOCK™ panel design for reduced drag and lower fuel costs
  • Aero Tarps & Custom Configs — Easy-open tarping systems and custom axle/suspension packages
