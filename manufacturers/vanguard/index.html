About Vanguard National Trailer Corporation

Vanguard National Trailer Corporation, headquartered in Monon, Indiana, manufactures a comprehensive range of dry freight vans, refrigerated trailers, flatbeds, converter dollies, and container chassis for the North American transportation industry. Every Vanguard trailer is engineered with premium performance features that deliver valuable operational savings and years of trouble-free service.

Vanguard’s dry van lineup — including the VXP Composite Plate, VIP 4000 Sheet & Post, and MaxCube — features industry-leading innovations such as the award-winning 30% Under-Ride Bracing with LED Glo Lights, fully galvanized rear frames, bolt-together bumpers, and galvanized steel thresholds. Their refrigerated trailers boast 12% greater thermal efficiency than competitors with triple rear door seals and high-quality foam insulation.

As an authorized Vanguard trailer dealer, Nationwide Haul offers the complete Vanguard product line — from composite plate dry vans to multi-temperature reefers — backed by competitive financing, expert consultation, and dedicated after-sale support for fleets of every size.

VXP — Composite Sidewall Van

The Vanguard VXP sets the standard for composite plate dry van trailers. Featuring snag-free composite plate panels with galvanized logistics posts, the VXP delivers 101¼” inside width panel to panel — maximizing cargo capacity while protecting freight from damage. Choose from standard or high base rail options to match your operation.

Every VXP ships with Vanguard’s award-winning 30% Under-Ride Bracing with integrated LED Glo Lights, fully galvanized rear frame with bolt-together bumper, and galvanized landing leg bracing across seven cross members for long-term corrosion resistance.

  • 101¼” inside width panel to panel
  • Snag-free composite plate sidewalls
  • Award-winning 30% Under-Ride Bracing
  • Fully galvanized rear frame & bumper

VIP 4000 — Sheet & Post Van

The Vanguard VIP 4000 is the go-to sheet and post dry van for fleets that need proven reliability and easy maintenance. Featuring 14 ga. 80 KSI Series “A” logistics posts on 16” centers throughout, with 100” inside width post to post and your choice of plastic or plywood interior lining, the VIP 4000 handles everything from palletized freight to retail distribution.

Standard features include the same galvanized rear frame, 30% Under-Ride Bracing, and dual-function LED lighting found across the entire Vanguard lineup — ensuring consistent quality and safety regardless of model.

  • 100” inside width post to post
  • 14 ga. 80 KSI logistics posts on 16” centers
  • Plastic or plywood interior lining
  • Dual-function LED clearance/turn lights

MaxCube — Anti-Snag Interior Van

The Vanguard MaxCube delivers 101¼” inside width with an innovative anti-snag snap-in interior liner that is completely impact-resistant and recessed between posts with zero fasteners — eliminating snag points that can damage freight or slow loading. The MaxCube uses Series “A” logistic posts on 16” centers throughout for consistent load securement.

Designed for high-volume operations that demand maximum interior space, fast loading cycles, and minimal freight damage, the MaxCube combines snag-free performance with the same galvanized construction and safety features that define every Vanguard trailer.

  • 101¼” inside width panel to panel
  • Anti-snag snap-in interior — zero fasteners
  • Impact-resistant plastic liner
  • Series “A” logistic posts on 16” centers

Refrigerated Trailers — Single & Multi-Temp

Vanguard refrigerated trailers are engineered to be 12% more thermally efficient than the competition. With triple rear door seals, high-quality foam insulation with smooth wall construction, and galvanized steel components throughout, these reefers maintain precise temperature control for food, pharmaceutical, and perishable freight — from truckload to grocery and multi-temp applications.

Standard features include bolt-on galvanized rear bumpers, reinforced overhead door frames, and tub-type cross members near the landing gear for enhanced structural integrity. Available with side doors for multi-stop distribution routes.

  • 12% greater thermal efficiency
  • Triple rear door seals
  • Single-temp, multi-temp & side-door options
  • Smooth wall foam insulation
