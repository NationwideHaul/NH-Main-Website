About XL Specialized Trailers

With over 100 years of heavy-haul design experience, XL Specialized Trailers is a leading designer and manufacturer of highly engineered and customized heavy-haul trailers in the USA. Headquartered in Manchester, Iowa and part of Caparo Bull Moose Industries, XL builds trailers that move the things that build and sustain the world — from construction equipment to wind turbine components.

Every XL trailer is custom-built to meet specific customer requirements, manufactured under ISO 9001:2015 certified lean processes. Their product line spans detachable gooseneck lowboys, extendable trailers, multi-axle configurations up to 170 tons, slide axle, hydraulic tail, wind energy trailers, and fully custom solutions — all backed by a 5-3-1 warranty (5-year structural, 3-year paint, 1-year components).

As an authorized XL Specialized dealer, Nationwide Haul offers the complete XL product line with competitive pricing, flexible financing, and comprehensive after-sale support for construction, energy, infrastructure, and heavy equipment operations.

Detachable Gooseneck Trailers

XL's detachable gooseneck lineup delivers hauling solutions for your heaviest loads. Available in mechanical and hydraulic configurations with low-profile decks, detachable front for drive-on loading, and multiple axle spacing options for state and provincial compliance.

Low-Profile HDG: Extra-low deck height for tall equipment
MFG — Mechanical Full-Width Gooseneck: Proven mechanical detach

Extendable Trailers

Purpose-built for oversized and long loads, XL extendable trailers feature telescoping deck sections with hydraulic or mechanical operation. Available in flat deck and step deck configurations with detachable gooseneck options for maximum versatility.

HDE — Hydraulic Detachable Extendable: Hydraulic extend & detach
MDE — Mechanical Detachable Extendable: Mechanical extend & detach
Flat Deck Extendable: Open deck for beams, poles & long cargo
Step Deck Extendable: Two-level deck for taller oversized loads

Multi-Axle, Trunnion & Specialty Trailers

For the heaviest and most challenging loads, XL offers multi-axle configurations scaling up to 170+ tons with jeep and booster combinations, trunnion-style axles, and steerable options. Plus specialty trailers for wind energy, slide-axle operations, and hydraulic tail loading.

Trunnion Style: Mechanical gooseneck with trunnion axles up to 170 tons
Expandable: Width-expanding for oversized machinery
Slide Axle: Adjustable axle positioning for weight distribution
Power Tail: Hydraulic tail for rear-loading without ramps
Folding Gooseneck: Hydraulic folding neck for versatile loading
