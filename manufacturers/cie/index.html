About CIE Manufacturing

CIE Manufacturing is the world's largest intermodal chassis manufacturer, producing the renowned Pioneer Chassis line. Every chassis is engineered for thousands of miles of reliable service, built at their American factories in Emporia, Virginia and South Gate, California, with additional production in Sarnia, Ontario.

The Pioneer Chassis line features robotic precision welds, laser metal cutting, and a proprietary KTL powder coat paint system that prevents rust and corrosion — ensuring your investment lasts. CIE offers flexible 5-year, 7-year, and 10-year warranty options for every chassis.

Nationwide Haul is proud to be an authorized CIE Manufacturing dealer, offering the full Pioneer Chassis product line with competitive pricing, flexible financing, and dedicated after-sale support for fleets of all sizes.

Tandem Container Chassis

CIE's Pioneer Tandem chassis are built to carry 20' and 40' ISO containers with unmatched durability. Designed for port drayage, intermodal yards, and over-the-road transport, every tandem chassis meets TOFC, AAR, DOT, FMVSS, ISO, ANSI, SAE, and TTMA specifications.

Models include: Pioneer 40' GN Tandem, Pioneer 40'/45' Extendable Tandem, Pioneer 23.5 Tandem, and Pioneer 20/40 CityCombo Tandem.

Tridem Container Chassis

For heavier loads and maximum weight distribution, CIE's Pioneer Tridem chassis deliver superior load capacity across three axles. Ideal for overweight containers, tank chassis, and drop frame applications.

Models include: Pioneer 20'/40' SL Combo Tridem, Pioneer 33' Lightweight Tridem, Pioneer 33' Slider Tridem, Pioneer 40' Gooseneck Tridem, and Pioneer 43' Drop Frame.
