About Wade Services Trailers

For over four decades, Wade Services, Inc. of Laurel, Mississippi has designed and manufactured premium steel platform and special application trailers for the most demanding industries in North America. From oil fields to poultry farms, highway freight to heavy equipment, Wade trailers are built tough for the jobs that push equipment to its limits.

At the heart of every Wade trailer is the patented MagniBeam™ — a proprietary I-beam technology developed in partnership with the Center for Advanced Vehicular Systems (CAVS) using high-performance computational design and state-of-the-art analytical tools. MagniBeam delivers superior structural integrity with Hi-Tensile ¼" one-piece webs and ½" thick 130K top and bottom flanges, resulting in trailers that handle extreme loads while maintaining a competitive tare weight.

As an authorized Wade Services trailer dealer, Nationwide Haul offers the complete Wade product lineup — including drop decks, flatbeds, live haul trailers, oil field floats, and specialty units — backed by competitive financing, expert consultation, and dedicated after-sale support.

Drop Deck Trailers — Standard & Deep Drop

Wade MagniBeam drop decks are available in all steel or aluminum/steel combination configurations in lengths from 35’ to 57’ and widths from 96” to 120” with unlimited options to match your application. Choose from a standard 12” drop or the deep 17” drop — introduced in 2013 for loads requiring extra vertical clearance.

Every drop deck features Wade’s signature MagniBeam construction with Hi-Tensile ¼" webs, ½" 130K flanges, and 4” 80-yield JR I-Beam cross members on 16” centers. Available configurations include tandem, tri-axle, spread axle, and air-ride suspensions.

  • All Steel or Aluminum/Steel Combo construction
  • 12” standard drop or 17” deep drop
  • Tandem, tri-axle & spread axle configurations
  • Optional container locks, ramps & toolboxes

Live Haul Trailers — Poultry & Livestock

The Wade MagniBeam live haul trailer was designed to conquer the harshest environments the poultry and livestock industry can throw at it. These specialized trailers feature Hi-Tensile ¼" one-piece webs and ½" thick 130K flanges with 3” inverted angles on 16” centers, X-bracing for extra lateral support during side loading, and inverted structural steel side rails for maximum durability.

Available in flatbed and drop deck configurations specifically engineered for poultry coops, livestock modules, and cage systems. Wade live haul trailers are trusted by major integrators and independent haulers across the Southeast and beyond.

  • X-Bracing for side-loading support
  • Inverted structural steel side rails
  • Flatbed & drop deck configurations
  • Built for poultry, turkey & livestock
