Our Story

Built to Simplify Commercial Equipment Ownership

Founded in 2012 by Derek and Tracy Madon, Nationwide Haul began as a small, relationship-driven used truck dealership built on industry knowledge and hard work. What started as a focused effort to serve owner-operators with reliable equipment quickly evolved into a full-service trucking ecosystem.

By recognizing that customers needed more than just trucks, Nationwide Haul expanded to offer financing, leasing, insurance, and service, creating a true one-stop shop designed to support drivers and fleets at every stage of their growth.
